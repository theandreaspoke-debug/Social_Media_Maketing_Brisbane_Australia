# Social Media Marketing & Consumer Purchasing Behaviour Analysis (2024)

## About This Project

My name is John, and I am a small business owner operating a fruit juice company in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia.

As digital marketing continues to shape consumer behaviour, I became interested in understanding how social media marketing can influence customer purchasing decisions and help small businesses grow online.

This independent data analysis project was created out of personal interest in:
- digital business growth,
- consumer behaviour,
- online marketing trends,
- and data-driven decision making for small businesses.

The project explores how social media platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook may influence customer engagement, trust, and purchasing behaviour among consumers in Brisbane.

---

## Purpose of the Analysis

The goal of this project is to better understand:
- which social media platforms attract the most engagement,
- how trust and online reviews affect purchasing decisions,
- what type of content consumers respond to most,
- and how small businesses can improve online marketing performance.

As a business owner planning to expand online marketing activities, this analysis was conducted to explore possible strategies that may improve customer reach and sales performance.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset used in this project is a simulated dataset generated in RStudio for educational and analytical purposes.

### Dataset Details
- Year represented: 2024
- Respondents: 150
- Location focus: Brisbane, Queensland, Australia
- Data type: Simulated consumer behaviour and social media engagement data

The dataset was designed to reflect realistic social media and consumer purchasing trends commonly discussed in digital marketing and retail research.

No real customer information was collected or stored.
